Composer, draft navigation and media viewer — 2026-10-20

Implemented in source:
- ComposerDrafts.cs: per-conversation session-only text, reply and attachment drafts.
  An unchanged retry keeps its submission ID; ACK identifies the submitted revision
  and never clears later input or another channel. The backend uses a <=25-character
  nonce derived from that ID; raw internal IDs remain 32 hexadecimal characters.
- TerminalWindow.Composer.cs: picker/file drop/bitmap paste/PNG-only paste prepare
  removable previews. Ctrl+V interception also works when WPF's text-only Paste
  command is unavailable. Nothing is transmitted until explicit Send/Enter.
- Prepared attachments are limited to 10 files, 25 MiB each, 100 MiB total. Original
  selected files are never deleted. Clipboard PNGs live in a private random session
  folder beneath the application's data directory and are cleared on discard,
  successful ACK, account change and normal shutdown.
- Permissions gate preparation and submission. Local removal is still available
  after attachment/send permissions change, so it cannot trap the retained text.
- TerminalWindow.NavigationContext.cs: cached channel return and bounded saved
  viewport positions. A return from an empty/interrupted view cannot leave a stale
  restoration flag that prevents future position capture.
- TerminalWindow.MediaViewer.cs: one viewer window, wheel zoom, pointer drag pan,
  fit/100% controls and bounded scroll offsets. GIF playback is explicit, shares the
  existing decoder and single-animation coordinator with chat, and stops on close.
- MediaDownloads.cs streams image/GIF originals to a user-selected destination,
  at most 25 MiB, with a 32 KiB buffer and at most 2 transfers. It validates supported
  origins and content types, uses no cookies or credentials, and cleans partial files.

Rendering/resource limits:
The enlarged static image uses the existing maximum 1600-pixel decoded preview;
saving retains the source bytes. GIF playback retains the existing 12 MiB download,
8 MiB decoded frame, 400-frame and native decoder dimension limits. Unsupported or
oversized animated media can still be opened in the original source. There is only
one enlarged viewer and one active GIF animation across chat and that viewer.
Up to 1024 nonempty draft slots are allowed; reaching the limit reports an error
instead of silently evicting unsent text. Drafts are intentionally not persisted.
